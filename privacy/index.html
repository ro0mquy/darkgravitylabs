Privacy

This page explains what personal data darkgravitylabs.com and work.darkgravitylabs.com process, why, and what rights you have under the GDPR.

No cookies, no analytics

Neither site sets cookies or uses analytics or advertising tools. Fonts and images are served from the sites themselves, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts or similar services.

darkgravitylabs.com

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub processes your IP address and request details to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub privacy statement. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

work.darkgravitylabs.com

The portfolio runs on my own server at Hetzner Online GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany. For each request the server logs the page or file, the time, the browser's user agent and referrer, and, instead of your IP address, only its country and network operator. It also stores two pseudonymous visitor hashes made with a key that changes every day and is never stored, so your IP address cannot be recovered from them. I use these logs to see which ads are viewed and to filter out bots. They are deleted after 12 months. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The site uses one small script that pauses a video when you start another; it sends nothing.

Booking a call

Call booking links go to Cal.com (Cal.com, Inc., USA). If you book, Cal.com processes the name, email and answers you enter and passes them to me so I can hold the call. See the Cal.com privacy policy. Legal basis: steps before a contract at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message to reply and to work with you. My mailbox is hosted by Fastmail. I keep business emails as long as tax and accounting rules require. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

Transfers outside the EU

GitHub and Cal.com are US companies. Transfers rely on the EU US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email me for any of these. You can also complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.
