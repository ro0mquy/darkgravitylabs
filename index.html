04

Keeping AI ads from looking like AI

You can usually tell an AI ad in a second, so here's what I do about it.

  • Real photographs as references for the look and style.
  • Every word gets checked, and the product against your real photos.
  • Each ad gets tested: would someone scrolling stop, and get the message?
  • I look at every ad myself before you see it.
