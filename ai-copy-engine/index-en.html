Key Findings

The "so what" — actionable insights that change how the client markets.

Finding 01
Checkfox runs zero customer-facing Facebook ads. All 10 ads are recruitment for "Inbound Closer" positions. Meanwhile, dentolo runs 25 and Clevox has been profitable for a year on just 33 page likes.
Finding 02
5 Trustpilot reviews (3.7/5) vs Check24’s 350,000 (4.8/5). Checkfox claims "TÜV 4.9/5" on their site. The gap between claimed and actual trust is a credibility risk.
Finding 03
dentolo’s reimbursement crisis since late 2025 is creating negative sentiment. Content opportunity: position against their weakness without naming them.
Finding 04
"Tarifdschungel" is the universal pain point — and Checkfox’s own tagline says "Dschungel lichten." They own this positioning but are not leveraging it in paid acquisition.
Finding 05
A practicing dentist endorses exactly what Checkfox sells ("GKV + gute Zahnzusatz = am besten versichert"). Dentist endorsement beats a Stiftung Warentest badge. Untapped partnership angle.
